About Flowmingo Studios

What We Do

Flowmingo Studios develops Vintake, a vehicle acquisition management system for dealerships, and Hairalyze, an AI-powered hair analysis app.

Vintake gives dealership teams a shared workspace for private-party acquisitions. Leads, appraisals, offers, appointments, and purchases stay connected, so the team can track each vehicle from first contact to purchase.
