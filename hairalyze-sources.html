Hairalyze Educational Sources

Hairalyze provides appearance-based cosmetic insights using visual indicators. This page collects educational sources users can review for general hair and scalp information.

Important Disclaimer

Hairalyze provides cosmetic and educational hair insights based on visual indicators. Hairalyze is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. For medical concerns such as sudden hair loss, scalp irritation, infection, persistent shedding, or scalp pain, consult a licensed dermatologist or healthcare professional.

These references are provided for educational review only. Hairalyze does not determine whether you have alopecia, scalp disease, infection, or another medical condition.

1. Hair Loss, Shedding, and Thinning

These sources explain common patterns of hair shedding, thinning, and hair loss, including when professional medical evaluation may be appropriate.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Hair loss overview

    General overview of common hair-loss patterns and causes from board-certified dermatology guidance.

  • Mayo Clinic: Hair loss symptoms and causes

    Medically reviewed explanation of symptoms, causes, and signs that may warrant medical follow-up.

  • Cleveland Clinic: Hair loss overview

    Overview of common causes of hair loss, including shedding, traction, hormonal changes, and processing damage.

  • MedlinePlus: Hair Loss

    NIH-supported reference hub with plain-language education and additional hair-loss resources.

2. Scalp Health and Irritation

These sources cover common scalp concerns such as dandruff, dry scalp, itching, flaking, and seborrheic dermatitis.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: How to treat dandruff

    Dermatologist-reviewed guidance on dandruff care and when to speak with a dermatologist.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Is your dry scalp something more serious?

    Educational overview comparing dandruff, dry scalp, and other scalp conditions.

  • Mayo Clinic: Dandruff symptoms and causes

    Medically reviewed explanation of dandruff symptoms, causes, and basic care.

  • Cleveland Clinic: Seborrheic dermatitis

    Overview of a common scalp condition associated with itching, flaking, and irritation.

3. Hair Breakage and Damage

These sources describe breakage, shaft damage, heat and chemical stress, and general ways to reduce cosmetic damage.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Tips for healthy hair

    Dermatologist guidance on washing, styling, and reducing everyday damage.

  • Cleveland Clinic: How to keep your hair healthy this winter

    Medically reviewed hair-care guidance covering breakage risk from tight styles, dryness, and seasonal stress.

  • PubMed Central: Effects of chemical straighteners on the hair shaft and scalp

    Peer-reviewed review article discussing shaft damage, scalp irritation, and risks associated with chemical straightening.

  • PubMed Central: Hair shaft damage from heat and drying time of hair dryer

    Peer-reviewed article on heat-related hair shaft damage and drying practices.

4. Dryness, Frizz, and Cosmetic Hair Care

These sources focus on appearance-related hair care, including dryness, frizz, protective routines, and environmental damage.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Tips for healthy hair

    General hair-care guidance that supports healthier-looking hair and gentler styling routines.

  • Cleveland Clinic: Can the sun damage your hair?

    Dermatologist-reviewed explanation of dryness, frizz, brittleness, and split ends linked to UV exposure.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Dry scalp conditions

    Helpful background on dryness and irritation that can affect the look and feel of hair and scalp.

  • PubMed Central: Hair structure, degradation, and conditioning review

    Peer-reviewed overview of how hair structure changes with wear, weathering, and cosmetic treatment.

5. General Hair and Scalp Education

These broader references provide medically reviewed or dermatologist-reviewed hair and scalp education for users who want more background.

  • American Academy of Dermatology: Hair care education

    Hair and scalp care guidance from the American Academy of Dermatology.

  • Mayo Clinic: Hair loss overview

    Medically reviewed overview of hair-loss symptoms, causes, and care context.

  • Cleveland Clinic: Hair loss education

    General educational resource on hair shedding, thinning, and contributing factors.

  • MedlinePlus: Hair loss resources

    NIH-backed reference collection with additional consumer health resources.
