Vintake

Keep your private-party acquisition pipeline moving

Launched June 2026

About Vintake

Vintake is a vehicle acquisition management system for dealership teams buying from private-party sellers. Bring appraisals, offers, appointments, and purchases into one shared vehicle record, so your team can see where each opportunity stands and what needs to happen next.

From First Contact to Purchased

  • Track each private-party lead through your acquisition pipeline
  • Keep appraisal values, condition details, offers, and notes together
  • Set appointments, assign drivers, and organize pickup details
  • See team outreach, purchase activity, and inventory statistics
